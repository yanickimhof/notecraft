# Changelog

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-24)
- Grok as a fourth AI engine.
- Actions on several selected lines at once (turn into, colour, group, move, delete).
- Columns by dragging a line beside another.
- Interface improvements and fixes.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-24)
- Dashboard view for databases and a graph view of the links between pages.
- Export a workspace as a website.
- Meetings: speakers told apart and renamed, custom vocabulary, a faster speech engine.
- ChatGPT as a third AI engine.
- Import check to spot what an import broke.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-23)
- Import from Evernote, Obsidian, Bear, Joplin, Word, HTML and CSV.
- Tabs, presentation mode and reminders.
- Formula and rollup properties, chart and timeline views.
- Meetings can record what the Mac plays (Teams, Zoom, Meet).

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-23)
- Meeting notes: local transcription, note templates, microphone choice.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-22)
- First release.
- Pages inside pages and a block editor, with links and backlinks.
- Databases with five views (table, board, list, calendar, gallery).
- Search, attachments, import and export (Markdown, HTML, PDF, Word).
- Private pages, automatic backups and page history.
- Local AI (Ollama) or Claude, and an assistant connection (MCP).
- Light and dark appearance, English and French.
