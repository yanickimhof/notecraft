# Fonts bundled in the PDF export

- Lato (Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic), SIL Open Font License 1.1: `crates/notecraft-core/fonts/OFL-Lato.txt`
- Inconsolata, SIL Open Font License 1.1: `crates/notecraft-core/fonts/OFL-Inconsolata.txt`

They are embedded (subsetted) in the PDF files NøteCraft produces. Characters the fonts do not
contain (emoji, some scripts) are left out of the PDF.
